
RETAIL SALES ANALYSIS PROJECT REPORT
====================================

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
This project analyzes retail sales data using Python
to understand sales performance, profitability,
regional trends, and customer segments.

2. TOOLS USED
- Python
- Jupyter Notebook
- Pandas and NumPy
- Matplotlib and Seaborn

3. DATASET INFORMATION
Total records after cleaning: 9994
Total columns: 25
Total sales: $2,297,200.86
Total profit: $286,397.02
Total orders: 5009
Total customers: 793

4. ANALYSIS PERFORMED
- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Sales by product category
- Profit by product category
- Monthly sales trends
- Regional sales analysis
- Top 10 products by sales
- Profit by customer segment
- Discount versus profit analysis

5. KEY FINDINGS
Best sales region:
West

Best-selling category by revenue:
Technology

Most profitable category:
Technology

Most profitable customer segment:
Consumer

6. CONCLUSION
The analysis helps identify sales trends and profitable
business areas. These insights can support better
inventory planning, marketing, and business decisions.

Note: Findings are based on the cleaned dataset.
